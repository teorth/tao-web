Bézout's theorem visualizer

Two plane curves of degree m and n meet in exactly mn points — if you count multiplicity, points at infinity, and complex points. Enter two integer-coefficient curves and watch the intersections, with each multiplicity drawn as a dot ringed by that many circles. The arithmetic is exact (multiplicity is a discrete invariant that floating point can't see).
