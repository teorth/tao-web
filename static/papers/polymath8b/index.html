Variants of the Selberg sieve, and bounded intervals containing many primes

Supplementary reference material and source files for the Polymath8b paper (arXiv:1407.4897, Research in the Mathematical Sciences 1:12, 2014).

LaTeX sources, compiled PDFs, and the Mathematica notebooks used for the numerical optimization (degree/monomial optimizers, region plots, variational computations), including the JM, MonomialMultiplier, Variational, and RIMS-submitted-version working directories. 48 files.

Build artifacts (logs, .aux/.bbl, editor backups) and the confidential referee reports are omitted; this is a curated snapshot of the working folder, not a complete mirror. Mathematica .nb files download rather than render in the browser.
